Experimental scripts of our paper ["Descent into Broken Trust: Uncovering ML-DSA Subkeys with Scarce Leakage and Local Optimization"](https://sacworkshop.org/SAC26/preproceedings/SAC2026_2-final46.pdf).
The experiments reported in the paper were carried out on an Apple MacBook equipped with an Apple M4 Max chipset and 128GB of RAM.
